# NLightning.Application — agent guide

Orchestration layer. It sits between the peer/transport layer (Infrastructure) and the domain plus crypto (Domain, Infrastructure.Bitcoin), and holds:
- PeerManager: the peer table, connect/accept, routing channel messages.
- ChannelManager: dispatches channel messages and reacts to blockchain events.
- The BOLT 2 v1 channel-open handlers and the BOLT 2 normal-operation receive handlers (update_add/fulfill/fail/fail_malformed_htlc, commitment_signed, revoke_and_ack, update_fee; BOLT2 plan N6-T1).
- MessageFactory: builds outbound protocol messages (Infrastructure's `PeerCommunicationService` still constructs error/warning messages directly).
- The send side (BOLT2 plan N6-T2): `IChannelOperations` (`ChannelOperationsService`), the commit scheduler and the `LocalOnlyHtlcSwitch`, which fails every incoming HTLC back once it is locked in (the default until `AddHtlcSwitchServices()` replaces it).
- The HTLC switch (ABCD W2-B): `Payments/Switch/HtlcSwitch` receives (final hop, invoices), forwards, propagates fulfills and failures upstream and replays after a restart. See "Payments" below.
- channel_reestablish (BOLT2 plan N7, ABCD W2-A): `Channels/Reestablish/` and `ChannelReestablishMessageHandler` (see below).
- Cooperative close (BOLT2 plan N10, legacy `closing_signed`): `Channels/Close/` and the `shutdown`/`closing_signed` handlers (see "Cooperative close" below).

- Channel safety (BOLT2 plan N9-T2/T4, ABCD W3): `Channels/Safety/` (HTLC deadline monitor, fail-the-channel broadcast). See "Channel safety" below.

- BOLT 5 wiring (ABCD W5): `Onchain/` (funding-spend watcher, resolution executor; see "BOLT 5 wiring" below) and the resolvers in `Onchain/Resolvers/`.

Not implemented yet: `option_simple_close` (N11); the rest of BOLT 5 (anchors O7, mempool O8; NL-094).

The payment core (ABCD W1-B) lives in `Payments/`: incoming onion processing, final hop, forwarding policy, route and onion building for our payments, and invoices. `Payments/Switch/` (W2-B) is the switch that calls it; the send path (`Payments/Send/PaymentService`, ABCD W2-C) uses it and offers our HTLCs through `IChannelOperations`. See "Payments" below.

## Layout
- `DependencyInjection.cs`: `AddApplicationServices()`. Registers the Domain services that have no DI of their own (IChannelOpenValidator, IChannelFactory, ICommitmentTransactionModelFactory, IFundingTransactionModelFactory; they need the host's IFeeService and AddBitcoinInfrastructure's ILightningSigner) and IChannelLockProvider, `ChannelManager` (built by a factory lambda, then exposed as both `IChannelManager` and `IChannelMessagePublisher`: one instance), IMessageFactory and IPeerManager as singletons, plus `AddCommitmentEngineServices()`, `AddChannelStateTransitionServices()` and `AddChannelOperationsServices()` (all in `Channels/Services/CommitmentEngineServiceCollectionExtensions.cs`), `Channels/Close/CloseServiceCollectionExtensions.AddChannelCloseServices()`, `Gossip.AddGossipServices()` and `Payments.AddPaymentsServices()`. A reflection scan registers every `IChannelMessageHandler<>` as Scoped. `FundingConfirmedMessageHandler` is registered as a concrete Scoped type. It is called from `src/NLightning.Daemon/Extensions/NodeServiceExtensions.cs`.
- `Channels/Handlers/Interfaces/IChannelMessageHandler.cs`: `Task<IReadOnlyList<IChannelMessage>> HandleAsync(msg, ChannelState currentState, FeatureOptions negotiatedFeatures, CompactPubKey peerPubKey)`. The returned messages are sent back to the same peer in list order (e.g. revoke_and_ack before commitment_signed); return `[]` for none.
- `Channels/Services/ChannelLockProvider.cs`: `IChannelLockProvider` (Domain) singleton, one non-reentrant `SemaphoreSlim` per channel id, dropped when unused. `AcquireAsync` / blocking `Acquire` return an `IDisposable`.
- `Channels/Handlers/`:
  - `OpenChannel1MessageHandler`: we are the non-initiator. Receives open_channel, replies accept_channel with **our** values (`ChannelParams.Local`, never the opener's; NL-194) and the opener's `channel_type` echoed byte for byte (BOLT 2, NL-218).
  - `AcceptChannel1MessageHandler`: we are the initiator. Receives accept_channel, fails it if `channel_type` differs from the one we sent (`ChannelParams.ToChannelType()`) or a reserve is below the other side's dust limit, stores the peer's values with `ChannelModel.UpdateRemoteParams` (our own, incl. `to_self_delay`, never change), builds the funding tx, replies funding_created. The channel is not persisted until funding_signed (BOLT 2: a funder that has not broadcast SHOULD NOT remember the channel).
  - `FundingCreatedMessageHandler`: non-initiator. Replies funding_signed, persists the channel and watches the funding tx.
  - `FundingSignedMessageHandler`: initiator. Signs the funding tx, then saves in **one** save the channel as V1FundingSigned, the funding watch, the signed funding tx as a pending `BroadcastTransactionModel` (purpose `Funding`) and the funding output's `WatchedOutpointModel` (BOLT 5 plan O0-T1/T2), tracks both watches in the monitor and publishes through `IChainBroadcaster.PublishAsync` (the monitor); a refused publish is only logged, because the monitor sends every stored pending tx again after each block and at startup (NL-258). `IChannelMemoryRepository.UpdateChannel` (the open subscription's V1FundingSigned event) is raised only after the publish. Sends no reply.
  - `ChannelReadyMessageHandler`: on the **first** channel_ready (the remote key set still at the first index) it builds the channel's first commitment snapshot (`ChannelStateTransitionService.CreateInitialCommitments`: balances, feerate, both commitment numbers, the peer's signature of our commitment 0, the peer's current point from the key set and the next point from the message) before the key set's point is overwritten (NL-232), and saves it with `IChannelStateDbRepository.InitializeAsync` in the same save as the channel; the model gets it only after the save. If the snapshot can't be built it logs and the channel works without HTLCs. Channels that received channel_ready before this existed have lost the peer's commitment-0 point and never get a snapshot (logged at startup; their HTLC messages get a warning).
  - Normal operation (N6-T1), all through the scoped `Services/ChannelStateTransitionService`: `UpdateAddHtlcMessageHandler`, `UpdateFulfillHtlcMessageHandler`, `UpdateFailHtlcMessageHandler`, `UpdateFailMalformedHtlcMessageHandler` (a failure_code without BADONION: warning + close, checked first), `UpdateFeeMessageHandler` (feerate bounds `MinAcceptableFeeratePerKw` 253 / `MaxAcceptableFeeratePerKw` 250,000), `CommitmentSignedMessageHandler` (replies revoke_and_ack, then our commitment_signed when changes are pending for the peer) and `RevokeAndAckMessageHandler` (inserts the secret into a fresh copy of the peer's shachain loaded from `IRemoteShachainDbRepository`, saves it with the transition as `ChannelStateExtras.RemoteShachain`, then signs if changes are pending). Each: guard (`GetUpdatableChannel`), engine call, `CommitAsync` (`ApplyAsync` + one `SaveChangesAsync`, then `ChannelModel.UpdateCommitments` + memory update, then the transition's events are queued), then the replies. Engine violations (`CommitmentViolationException`) become `ChannelWarningException { CloseConnection = true }` (BOLT 2's warning-and-close option; the engine state is unchanged and reestablish recovers), except `MustFailChannel` ones (bad revocation secret, B2-RAA-R01) which become `ChannelFailedException`.
  - `FundingConfirmedMessageHandler`: not an IChannelMessageHandler. It is triggered by a funding confirmation and emits channel_ready through its `OnMessageReady` event.
- `Channels/Services/CommitmentSigningService.cs` (BOLT2 plan N3-T5): a concrete service (no interface) with `SignRemoteCommitment(channel, CommitmentTxSpec, remoteNumber, remotePoint)` and `VerifyLocalCommitment(channel, CommitmentTxSpec, localNumber, sig, htlcSigs)` (throws `SignerException`), both returning `CommitmentTxSignatures(txid, sig, htlcSigs)`. It runs factory (spec-driven overload) -> `BuildWithOutputMap` -> `HtlcTransactionModelFactory` -> `IHtlcTransactionBuilder.Build` -> `ILightningSigner` (`SignChannelTransaction` + `SignRemoteHtlcTransactions`, or `ValidateSignature` + `ValidateLocalHtlcSignatures`), so HTLC signatures are always in commitment output order. Vectors: `test/NLightning.Integration.Tests/BOLT3/CommitmentSigningServiceVectorTests` (Appendix C/F: as node B the signatures equal `remote_signature`/`remote_htlc_signature`; as node A the vector signatures verify).
- Engine ports (NL-230), same folder: `EngineCommitmentSignerPort : ICommitmentSigner` and `EngineCommitmentVerifierPort : ICommitmentVerifier` (the Domain engine ports in `Channels/Commitments/Interfaces`) look the channel up in `IChannelMemoryRepository` by id (static data only), adapt the engine's `CommitmentSpec` with `CommitmentTxSpec.FromCommitmentSpec`, and call `CommitmentSigningService`; they reject a spec of the wrong holder (`ArgumentException`) and an unknown channel (`InvalidOperationException`). The verifier maps `SignerException` to `false` (the engine then raises B2-CS-R01) and logs it; other exceptions propagate. `EngineRevocationVerifierPort : IRevocationVerifier` delegates to `IPerCommitmentSecretVerifier.Verify`. All four are singletons registered by `CommitmentEngineServiceCollectionExtensions.AddCommitmentEngineServices()` (called from `AddApplicationServices`). Proof: `test/NLightning.Application.Tests/Channels/Services/EngineCommitmentPortsTwoNodeTests` drives two engines with real `LocalLightningSigner`s built from the production DI (`RealSigningCommitmentPair`) through add/CS/RAA/fulfill/fail/update_fee, with and without anchors, and checks that the txid each side signed equals the txid the other verified; tampered commitment/HTLC signatures fail B2-CS-R01 and a wrong secret B2-RAA-R01. The same harness records each node's domain events (`RealSigningNode.Events`, N4-T4) and checks lock-in once on revoke_and_ack and fail/settle only when irrevocable. The N6-T1 receive handlers drive the engine through `ChannelStateTransitionService`. `ChannelManager` hands the `IHtlcSwitch` (Domain, `Channels/Interfaces`; `LocalOnlyHtlcSwitch` by default) the events of each persisted transition, and replays `ChannelDomainEvents.DerivePending` at startup.
- `Channels/Services/ChannelStateTransitionService.cs` (scoped, N6-T1): `GetUpdatableChannel(id, state, name)` is the state guard (HTLCs disabled by `NodeOptions.HtlcsEnabled` → the old "not supported yet" warning; channel not in memory or without a snapshot → warning; `Failed` → `error` again; not `Open` → warning + close, B2-NO-02); `CommitAsync(channel, result, extras)` persists one transition (D3/I1/I2); `CreateRevokeAndAck` calls `ILightningSigner.AdvanceLocalCommitment` then `RevealPerCommitmentSecret` (only after the save, I3); `SignIfPendingAsync` signs the peer's next commitment when `ChannelCommitments.CanSendCommit` (never after data loss) and saves it with `ChannelStateExtras.SentCommitDiff` (our pending updates rebuilt from the snapshot — adds by id, removals by id, fee — then the commitment_signed, in `SentCommitDiffCodec` format: u16 big-endian length + serialized message each) and `LastSent`; `ToWireMessage` maps engine outbound messages to wire messages; `LoadRemoteShachainAsync` gives a fresh store loaded from the database. `ChannelDomainEventQueue` (scoped) collects the events of the persisted transitions. `SecretStorageServiceFactory` (`ISecretStorageServiceFactory`, `TryAddSingleton`) makes `SecretStorageService` instances. All registered by `AddChannelStateTransitionServices()` (in `CommitmentEngineServiceCollectionExtensions`, called from `AddApplicationServices`).
- Channels without a snapshot (channel_ready handled before ABCD wave 1, NL-246) cannot be given one: the first snapshot needs the peer's per-commitment point of its current commitment 0, which that channel_ready replaced with the second point, and nothing we store yields it (we keep no remote commitment 0 transaction, and a signature does not reveal the key). `channel_reestablish` is no way back: BOLT 2 now says the receiver MUST ignore `my_current_per_commitment_point` (the sender only has to send a valid point). Such a channel keeps working without HTLCs (HTLC messages get a warning); close and reopen it once N10 exists, and never build a snapshot from an unverified point.
- Send side (N6-T2), `Channels/Interfaces/` and `Channels/Services/`:
  - `ChannelOperationsService : IChannelOperations` (singleton): Offer/Fulfill/Fail/FailMalformed/UpdateFee each take the channel's lock, check the preconditions (`NodeOptions.HtlcsEnabled`, `Open`, not `Failed`, no data loss, a snapshot, and for **every** operation the channel's link is up: `IPeerLivenessProbe.IsAliveAsync(channelId, peer)`), run the engine (its sender rules throw `CommitmentRefusedException`), persist with `ChannelStateTransitionService.CommitAsync` (one save; its optional `stageWithTransition` callback stages more writes on the same unit of work before that save: the offer's `HtlcOrigin`, and the caller's writes of the `FulfillHtlcAsync` overload with `stageWithFulfill`), publish the wire message through `IChannelMessagePublisher` under the lock, then `ICommitScheduler.Schedule` after the lock. Nothing is persisted or sent on a refusal; an unknown channel throws `KeyNotFoundException`. A removal or fee update for a peer that is away (or on a connection the channel was not opened/reestablished on) is refused like an offer: `PeerManager` drops messages for a peer that is not connected and nothing retransmits before N7, so a persisted-but-unsent update would later be covered by a `commitment_signed` the peer can't verify. The HTLC stays locked in and the switch acts again on the replay (startup, and with `AddHtlcSwitchServices` every `MarkLinkUp`, see the HTLC switch section; N7 must call `MarkLinkUp` after the reestablish). Left for N7: an update enqueued just as its connection closes is lost with that connection (the link is then down for good, so nothing signs over it; N7's reestablish must forget/retransmit it). `RecordOnionSecretAsync` stores the incoming HTLC's Sphinx secret (`IChannelStateDbRepository.SetOnionSharedSecretAsync`, one save). The `HtlcOrigin` of an offer is validated and staged with `IChannelStateDbRepository.SetHtlcOriginAsync` in the add's save (NL-250), so a restart routes the outgoing HTLC's resolution back to its circuit or payment. "channel_reestablish processed" is approximated by the pinned link (N7 must call `MarkLinkUp`).
  - `CommitScheduler : ICommitScheduler` (singleton, `CommitSchedulerOptions.Debounce`, default 10 ms): one waiting round per channel, run on the thread pool without the caller's execution context (so its message goes to the peer's current connection); `SignNowAsync` asks `IPeerLivenessProbe` (ping-before-commit) before the lock and again under it (a reconnection meanwhile must not get a commitment_signed before channel_reestablish), then under the lock `ChannelStateTransitionService.SignIfPendingAsync` (never while `RemoteNextCommit` exists, D7; persisted with the diff) and publishes. `WhenIdleAsync` for tests. A failed signature is logged; the changes stay pending. The receive handlers still sign on their own after commitment_signed/revoke_and_ack.
  - `IPeerLivenessProbe` → `ConnectedPeerLivenessProbe` (singleton): channel-scoped. `MarkLinkUp(channelId, peer)` pins the peer's current connection (the `PeerModel` instance `IPeerManager.GetPeer` returns; one per connection); `IsAliveAsync(channelId, peer)` is true only while `GetPeer` still returns that same instance, so after a reconnection the channel is down until marked again. `ChannelManager` marks a channel when it turns `Open` (channel_ready handled, or our funding confirmation); a channel loaded at startup or reconnected is marked after its channel_reestablish (N7), and the production registration gates the probe with the `ReestablishTracker` (`ReestablishGatedLivenessProbe`). Without a peer manager (in-process tests) a marked channel is up. The peer manager is resolved lazily. The BOLT 2 ping before `commitment_signed` is not the probe's job: `CommitScheduler.SignNowAsync` asks `IPingBeforeCommit` (`PingBeforeCommit`, singleton, `TryAdd`ed by `AddChannelOperationsServices`) after the cheap link check and before taking the lock; it pings through `IPeerService.PingAsync` when `IPeerService.LastMessageReceivedAt` is older than `CommitSchedulerOptions.PingWhenQuietFor` (30 s) and waits `PongTimeout` (10 s). No pong: nothing is signed and the connection is closed (BOLT 1 MAY), so the next connection's reestablish signs the pending changes; without a peer manager (in-process harnesses) every peer counts as responsive (NL-251).
  - `IChannelMessagePublisher`: implemented by `ChannelManager.Publish` (raises `OnResponseMessageReady`); call it under the channel's lock. It drops normal-operation messages (128-135) of a channel that is not reestablished on the peer's current connection (the connection can be replaced during the caller's save; the reestablish retransmits what was persisted), checked and raised under the same gate as `OnPeerConnectionChanged`, which `PeerManager` calls before it swaps the session, so nothing precedes our channel_reestablish on a new connection (B2-RE-07).
  - `Channels/Switch/LocalOnlyHtlcSwitch : IHtlcSwitch` (`TryAddSingleton`; replace it with `services.Replace(...)`): on `IncomingHtlcLockedIn` it skips an HTLC that is no longer in `RcvdAddAckRevocation` (idempotent replay), fails a blinded HTLC with `invalid_onion_blinding` (malformed), peels with `ISphinxService.PeelAsLocalNode(packet, payment_hash)` (BADONION → `update_fail_malformed_htlc` with the exception's sha256_of_onion; `invalid_onion_payload` with a secret → encrypted), records the shared secret, then fails with `incorrect_or_unknown_payment_details(htlc_msat, IBlockchainMonitor.LastProcessedBlockHeight)` when final and the height is known, or `temporary_node_failure` when it would forward or no block was processed yet (height 0 would mislead the payer); no BOLT 4 final-node checks yet (`final_incorrect_cltv_expiry`/`final_incorrect_htlc_amount`, W2-B), encrypted by `IFailureOnionService.CreateErrorPacket`. No replay-cache entry. Outgoing fulfilled/failed are logged; `OutgoingHtlcSettled` prunes the archived row (`PruneSettledHtlcsAsync`, one save, NL-243). A `CommitmentRefusedException`/`KeyNotFoundException` is logged, not thrown.
- `Channels/Managers/ChannelManager.cs`: the `switch (message.Type)` in `DispatchChannelMessageAsync`. `HandleChannelMessageAsync` creates the message scope **before** taking the lock; after the lock is released it drains the scope's `ChannelDomainEventQueue` into the registered `IHtlcSwitch` (a failing switch is logged, the events are re-derivable), also when the handler threw after a persisted transition. A `ChannelFailedException` from a handler is persisted first (under the lock): `ChannelState.Failed` and the serialized `error` (`ChannelModel.MarkErrorSent`) through `ChannelDbRepository.UpdateAsync` + save (skipped when the channel is already Failed with a stored error), then rethrown (PeerManager sends the error and keeps the connection). funding_created runs under its temporary id's lock **and** the real channel id's lock (computed with `IChannelIdFactory`, temporary first; NL-235): the only place two locks are held. `RegisterExistingChannelAsync` first resumes the stored state (N7-T5): Closed/Stale and pre-funding states (None, V1Opening, V2Opening) are not registered; a funder's `V1FundingCreated` (only from builds before BOLT 5 plan O0, which saved the channel before the publish and V1FundingSigned after it; the handler now saves V1FundingSigned with the funding tx in one save) becomes V1FundingSigned when a watched transaction with its funding txid and channel id is stored (the watch is saved before the publish, so the tx may be out) and is persisted Stale and not registered otherwise (BOLT 2: a funder that has not broadcast SHOULD NOT remember; NL-048 wontfix, its locked UTXOs are not released); V1FundingSigned, ReadyFor*, ShuttingDown, Negotiating, Closing (see "Cooperative close") and Failed are registered as they are. It also keeps `Failed` channels in memory, and for a channel with a snapshot runs `ChannelCommitments.RevertUncommitted` and persists it when it changed something (before any reestablish); for an `Open` channel it then derives the pending events (`ChannelDomainEvents.DerivePending` over the snapshot and the unpruned settled rows from `IChannelStateDbRepository.LoadAsync`; a failure is logged and skipped) and hands them to the switch **after** releasing the lock (I8). At startup no channel's link is up, so a replayed fail is refused (logged by the switch) and the HTLC stays locked in until the replay after N7's reestablish. `HandleChannelMessageAsync` (a channel that was not `Open` before the message and is after it) and the funding confirmation (after `FundingConfirmedMessageHandler`) call `IPeerLivenessProbe.MarkLinkUp` under the lock. Also handles blockchain events: `HandleNewBlockDetected` (ForgetStaleChannels, ConfirmUnconfirmedChannels) and `HandleFundingConfirmationAsync`.
- `Node/Managers/PeerManager.cs`: `ConcurrentDictionary<CompactPubKey, PeerSession>`; a session is one connection: the `PeerModel`, a bounded inbound queue (1024, the transport read loop waits when full) drained by one loop, a close token, and the connection's `PeerOutbox`. A connection becomes a session only after `IPeerService.WaitForInitAsync` (both inits exchanged; its preferred address and features come from that init): `ConnectToPeerAsync` returns only then and throws `ConnectionException` (after disposing the peer service) if the connection closes first, and an inbound connection is handled on its own task (`HandleNewPeerConnectedAsync`) and dropped with an info log in that case. So neither end ever keeps a connection the other cannot use (NL-240). `StopAsync` sets `_stopping` under the session lock and cancels the pending init waits (`WaitForInitAsync` gets a stopping token; the connection is disconnected and disposed); `TryInstallSession` refuses under the same lock once stopping (`InstallResult.Stopping`), so every installed session is in the disconnect pass, and `StopAsync` also waits (5 s timeout) for the inbound setups still running (`_inboundSetups`, registered before any of the setup runs), so no peer is saved to the database after it returns. Sessions subscribe to the peer service right after that (before any DB work; `PeerService` replays channel messages and a disconnection that happened before), and only then are installed. Closing a session cancels its loop (queued messages are dropped, the one being handled finishes); the next session of the same peer starts its loop only after the previous loop ended, and `StopAsync` waits for all loops. Replies raised while a loop handles a message go to that loop's session (an `AsyncLocal`), so they are dropped rather than sent on a newer connection. A new connection replaces the current one, except within `SimultaneousConnectWindow` (5 s) of an opposite-direction connection, where the connection initiated by the lower pubkey is kept (LND's `shouldDropLocalConnection`). A runtime drop of a peer with active channels in `IChannelMemoryRepository` starts the backoff reconnect loop unless the disconnect was ours (`DisconnectPeer`, `StopAsync`, replacement). It subscribes to `IChannelManager.OnResponseMessageReady` in its constructor and only enqueues there (never throws). Maps exceptions (through the outbox, so they follow the replies queued before them), always scoped to the message's channel id (never an all-zero `error`, BOLT 1): ChannelFailedException sends `error` for the channel and keeps the connection (N7), any other ChannelErrorException sends `error` for the channel and disconnects, ChannelWarningException sends a `warning` and stays connected (or disconnects after it when `CloseConnection` is set), any other exception (our bug) sends a channel `warning` and disconnects.
- `PeerManager.StartAsync` (NL-201): awaits `IChannelManager.RegisterExistingChannelAsync` (takes the channel lock; memory + signer) for every non-Closed/Stale channel of every stored peer before connecting to any of them; a failing channel is logged and skipped. A peer with such channels that can't be reached gets a background reconnect loop (`NodeOptions.ReconnectInitialDelay`, default 5 s, doubling up to `NodeOptions.ReconnectMaxDelay`, default 10 min; config `Node:ReconnectInitialDelay`/`Node:ReconnectMaxDelay`; `PeerManager` takes an optional `IOptions<NodeOptions>` and falls back to those defaults without it) that ends when the peer is connected (either direction) or `StopAsync` cancels it (StopAsync waits for the loops before disconnecting peers).
- `Channels/Fees/` (BOLT2 plan N9-T1/N9-T3, ABCD W3-C): `FeeUpdatePolicy` (pure: funder only; target = estimate (times `NonAnchorFeerateMarginPercent`, default 200, without anchors: the BOLT 2 "significant margin") clamped to [`Node:FeeUpdates:MinFeeratePerKw` >= 253, `MaxFeeratePerKw` 50,000 / `MaxAnchorFeeratePerKw` 2,500 with anchors]; moves only when the target differs from `ChannelCommitments.LatestFeeratePerKw` by >= `ThresholdPercent` (20) or the feerate is below the minimum; an increase must pass the engine's `SendFee` (B2-FEE-R03, our reserve) and, without anchors, the dust limit at the new feerate (B2-DUST-05), else the highest sendable feerate in between is used (binary search) or nothing), `DustExposurePolicy` (pure: `Resolve(commitments, nodeLimit)` = the snapshot's `CommitmentParams.MaxDustHtlcExposureMsat` ?? `NodeOptions.MaxDustHtlcExposureMsat` (the fallback covers only the receive and fee checks: the engine's send rules B2-DUST-03/04 read the stored value, so a snapshot without one offers unlimited dust until it is backfilled); `ProspectiveExposureMsat`, `CheckFeeIncrease`, and `CheckLockedInIncoming` = B2-DUST-01/02 for a locked-in incoming HTLC, counting only the outgoing HTLCs and the incoming ones with a lower id, so replays give the same answer), `FeeUpdateScheduler : IFeeUpdateScheduler` (singleton; `StartAsync` runs a round every `Node:FeeUpdates:Interval` (10 min) unless `Enabled` is false, `RunOnceAsync` runs one now and returns a `FeeUpdateOutcome` per channel we fund (`Open`, snapshot, no data loss); estimate from `IFeeService.GetFeeRatePerKwAsync` (0 or an exception: nothing); sends through `IChannelOperations.UpdateFeeAsync`, so the commit scheduler signs it; refusals are logged and retried next round) and `DustExposureHtlcSwitch` (an `IHtlcSwitch` decorator: an `IncomingHtlcLockedIn` over the dust limit is failed with `temporary_channel_failure` (onion peeled with `replayOwner: null` (no replay check) for the shared secret; a malformed onion is left to the switch) before the switch can forward it or reveal the preimage; never when the switch already stored the HTLC's onion secret or a forward circuit; serialized per incoming HTLC). `ChannelFeeServiceCollectionExtensions.AddChannelFeeServices()` registers the scheduler and decorates the registered switch (idempotent; call it after `AddHtlcSwitchServices()`); the host must call `IFeeUpdateScheduler.StartAsync` after `PeerManager.StartAsync` and `StopAsync` on shutdown. The send side of the dust rule (B2-DUST-03/04) is the engine's (`UpdateValidator`, from the snapshot's params); `ChannelReadyMessageHandler` stores `NodeOptions.MaxDustHtlcExposureMsat` with the first snapshot (`ChannelStateTransitionService.CreateInitialCommitments(..., maxDustHtlcExposureMsat)`, NL-254). Tests: `test/NLightning.Application.Tests/Channels/Fees/` (policy tables, scheduler with mocks, `FeeUpdateHarnessTests` on `TwoNodeHarness` incl. a crossing add, `DustExposureHtlcSwitchTests`, `FirstSnapshotDustPolicyTests`); Docker `Docker/FeeUpdateFlowTests`.
- `Gossip/` (ABCD W1-E, NL-099 subset: direct `channel_update` exchange on our unannounced channels): `Interfaces/IChannelUpdateService`, `Services/ChannelUpdateService` (singleton), `Events/ChannelUpdateReadyEventArgs`, and `GossipServiceCollectionExtensions.AddGossipServices()` (TryAdd, so idempotent; called from `AddApplicationServices`). `PeerManager` takes it as an optional last constructor parameter: without it nothing is sent or stored. The service subscribes to `IChannelMemoryRepository.OnChannelUpdated`; the first time a channel is seen `Open` with a short channel id it runs `SendChannelUpdateAsync` on another task, which takes the channel's lock first (the event is raised under the lock by the handler that opened the channel, before it queues its own channel_ready, so our update always follows channel_ready in the outbox), builds and signs the update (`must_be_one|dont_forward`, `direction` = 1 when our node id is the greater, the real scid or, for an `option_scid_alias` channel (`ChannelParams.UseScidAlias` > No), the peer's alias `RemoteAlias` (BOLT 2 forbids routing into it by the real scid), fee/CLTV delta from `NodeOptions.Routing`, `htlc_minimum_msat` = max(peer's htlc_minimum, `Routing.HtlcMinimumMsat`), `htlc_maximum_msat` = min(capacity, peer's max_htlc_value_in_flight, `Routing.HtlcMaximumMsat`), timestamp = UNIX time but always above the previous one; no update at all, with a warning log, when the minimum is above that maximum or an alias channel has no `RemoteAlias` yet: `CreateChannelUpdate` throws `InvalidOperationException` there) and raises `OnChannelUpdateReady`, which `PeerManager` turns into `PeerOutbox.TryEnqueueGossip`. `HandleRemoteChannelUpdate(peer, message)` keeps the peer's update only if it is for our chain, names a channel with that peer (real scid, our `LocalAliases` or its `RemoteAlias`), has the peer's direction, verifies with the peer's node id (`ILightningSigner.VerifyNodeMessage`), is newer, is at most `ChannelUpdateService.MaxFutureTimestamp` (14 days) ahead of our clock and has `htlc_maximum_msat` <= capacity; `TryGetRemoteChannelUpdate`/`TryGetLocalChannelUpdate` read them (for route hints and BOLT 4 UPDATE failures). Resend: every session `PeerManager` installs (after init; also after a reconnect or a restart) runs `SendChannelUpdatesToPeerAsync(peer)` on another task: for each `Open` channel with that peer (under its channel lock) the last update again if its policy is still current (same timestamp, as LND does on reconnect), else a new one. ## Cooperative close (BOLT2 plan N10: N10-T1..T3)
- States: `Open` → `ShuttingDown` (23, a `shutdown` sent or received; persisted with the script) → `Negotiating` (25, both scripts known and `ChannelCommitments.IsCleared`: no HTLC, every fee update final, no revoke_and_ack owed) → `Closing` (30, the agreed fully signed closing tx persisted in `ChannelModel.ClosingTransaction` before it is sent or broadcast) → `Closed` (40, when the watched closing tx reaches `ChannelCloseOptions.ConfirmationDepth`, default 6; `ChannelManager.CompleteCloseAsync` persists it and removes the channel from memory). Scripts and the closing tx are columns of `Channels` (migration `AddShutdownState`).
- `Channels/Close/ChannelCloseCoordinator` (scoped, runs under the channel's lock, returns the messages to send in wire order): `InitiateAsync` (IPC: signs our pending updates first with `ChannelStateTransitionService.SignIfPendingAsync`, B2-SHUT-S03; refuses when they wait for a revoke_and_ack; only Open channels; once only), `ReceiveShutdownAsync` (script form per `Domain/Channels/Closing/ShutdownScriptValidator`, anysegwit/simple_close from the negotiated features: warning, state unchanged, B2-SHUT-R02; the peer's upfront script when `option_upfront_shutdown_script` was negotiated: warning + close, B2-SHUT-R05; a second shutdown with another script: warning + close; before channel_ready: warning (the MAY of B2-SHUT-R03 is not implemented); in Closing: ignored; replies with ours once none of our updates is unsigned, signing them first), `AdvanceAsync` (our deferred shutdown, the move to Negotiating, and, for the funder, the opening `closing_signed` once both shutdowns went over the **current** connection), `ReceiveClosingSignedAsync` (fee above the funder's balance or an inverted range: warning + close; the signature must verify for one variant (outputs trimmed by the peer's dust limit, the same without the peer's output, or trimmed by ours; B2-CLS-R01); any output below its script's BOLT 3 dust threshold fails the channel (B2-CLS-R10); then the pure `LegacyClosingNegotiator` decides: propose, agree (sign the variant the peer signed, persist Closing, echo when due, with the closing tx's `WatchedTransactionModel` staged in the **same** save as Closing (then `IBlockchainMonitor.TrackWatchedTransaction` + `PublishTransactionAsync`; a failed broadcast is logged), warn or fail), in Closing a peer's `closing_signed` (it restarted the negotiation after our final one was lost) is answered with our signature of the stored tx at its fee (a different fee: always; From the first shutdown on, every proposal we sign can be broadcast, so the funding outpoint is watched for a spend (`IBlockchainMonitor.WatchOutpointSpend`; ChannelManager re-registers it at startup for ShuttingDown/Negotiating/Closing). Fee: the IPC request's feerate, else the estimate of `ClosingFeeEstimator` (singleton, `AddChannelCloseServices`; one `IFeeService.GetFeeRatePerKwAsync` fetch at a time, the last good value kept for `Node:Close:FeeEstimateMaxAge` (10 min), a failed fetch (exception or 0) not retried before `FeeEstimateRetryAfter` (1 min)) read once per negotiation (per connection) into `ClosingNegotiationRegistry.Entry.EstimateFeeratePerKw` (the host's fee service is a transient typed HttpClient, so the cached value alone was always 0 and every close proposed the floor, W4-E). Under the channel lock only the first message of a negotiation waits for a fetch, at most `FeeEstimateWaitUnderLock` (2 s; `Entry.EstimateAttempted`), later ones take what the estimator has; the IPC close prefetches before taking the lock and a received `shutdown` starts a fetch without waiting (W4-E review F3); without an estimate the fee service's cached value, then the floor. Floor 253 sat/kw, × `ClosingFeeCalculator.EstimateWeight`; the funder accepts up to `ChannelCloseOptions.MaxFeeMultiplier` (3) × its estimate, the non-funder anything from the relay floor up to the funder's balance. `fee_range` is sent unless `ChannelCloseOptions.SendFeeRange` (`Node:Close:SendFeeRange`) or the IPC request turns it off; a peer that sends none is answered without one too.
- `ClosingNegotiationRegistry` (singleton): per channel, what happened on the current connection (shutdown sent/received), the running `ClosingNegotiation` (memory only; `ChannelManager.OnPeerConnectedAsync` resets it: BOLT 2 restarts the negotiation on reconnection, B2-RE-29), the IPC request, the waiters for the closing tx and the two closing deadlines (`ReplyDueAt`, dropped by a new connection; `FeeRangeDueAt`, kept across reconnections).
- Closing timeouts (NL-284, ABCD W4-E): `ClosingTimeoutMonitor` (singleton, `AddChannelCloseServices`, optional `TimeProvider`) runs one timer per channel. The coordinator arms `ReplyDueAt` = now + `Node:Close:ClosingSignedReplyTimeout` (5 min) whenever it sends a `closing_signed` that needs an answer (the funder's opening one, every proposal) and clears it on any `closing_signed` received, on agreement, on a new connection and when the channel fails out of the negotiation (B2-CLS-R10 or a negotiator failure, which also clear `FeeRangeDueAt`); the reply deadline only counts while the peer is on the connection the channel's link is pinned to (`IPeerLivenessProbe`, checked when the timer fires): a peer that dropped after our `closing_signed` is not failed for it (W4-E review F1; checked before the lock, so a drop in the instant between the check and the lock is not covered); a `fee_range` without overlap (the R04 warning) arms `FeeRangeDueAt` = first such range + `FeeRangeTimeout` (10 min), cleared by any non-warning outcome (an overlapping range, a closing_signed without range, agreement). - Wiring: `ChannelManager` dispatches `shutdown`/`closing_signed` (gated like normal-operation messages until the channel is reestablished) and calls `AdvanceAsync` after every handled message of a ShuttingDown/Negotiating channel (a failure there is logged, never sent to the peer). - `HtlcExpiryMonitor : IHtlcExpiryMonitor`: on `IBlockchainMonitor.OnNewBlockDetected` (rounds on the thread pool, never overlapping, coalesced to the latest height; `CheckAsync(height)` runs one round; `WhenIdleAsync` for tests) it evaluates every HTLC of every loaded `Open`/`Failed` channel with a snapshot, outside every lock. ## BOLT 5 wiring (ABCD W5, plan O2-T5, O6-T2; `Onchain/`)
Registered by `OnchainServiceCollectionExtensions.AddOnchainServices()` (TryAdd singletons, from `AddApplicationServices` after `AddChannelSafetyServices`); `OnchainOptions` from `Node:Onchain` (`ReasonableDepth` 6 for the resolvers, `IrrevocableDepth` 100). After the lock: `TrackWatchedOutpoint`, the error through `IChannelErrorSender` (B5-GEN-04), critical alerts (B5-GEN-06 unknown, B5-RMT-03 future, B5-GEN-06 for vouts the mapper could not identify (`CommitmentOutputMap.UnmappedVouts`, with amounts; "predates the revocation log" for a revoked number below `GetLogStartAsync`)), `IOnchainResolutionExecutor.CatchUpSpendsAsync(new watches, spend height)` (the monitor keeps processing blocks while the spend is classified: an output spent in the commitment's block or a block processed before the watch was tracked is found by scanning those blocks), then `ResolveChannelAsync(spend height)`. ## BOLT 5 local commitment resolution (plan O3-T3/T4; `Onchain/Resolvers/`)
- `LocalCommitResolver : IOutputResolver` (Domain port, W5-A; `CanResolve(LocalCommitment)`) resolves **our** commitment on chain. Stateless: each `ResolveAsync(close, rows, height)` / `OnOutputSpentAsync(close, row, spender, height)` opens a scope, reloads the channel with its snapshot (`ChannelDbRepository.GetByIdAsync`), rebuilds our commitment with `ICommitmentOutputMapper` (the rebuilt txid must equal `ChannelCloseModel.CommitmentTransactionId`, and the close's number our latest local number, else it logs and does nothing), reads each output's spend from `WatchedOutpointDbRepository` (spender txid/height; "by us" = the row's `ResolvingTransactionId` or a `BroadcastTransactions` row of the channel) and runs the pure `OutputResolutionPlanner`. It only returns actions (the executor stages them in one save, then publishes, watches, raises, alerts): `UpsertOutputAction` (rows for our outputs the watcher did not record, `Waiting` + `WaitUntilHeight`, `Broadcast` + `ResolvingTransactionId`, `Ignored` for an expired peer HTLC we may not claim or a dust sweep), `BroadcastAction` (HTLC-timeout/success purpose `HtlcTransaction`: the peer's stored HTLC signature from `LocalCommit.RemoteSignatures` by the HTLC's rank among the HTLC outputs in vout order + `ILightningSigner.SignLocalHtlcTransaction` + `IHtlcTransactionBuilder.AddWitness`; sweeps purpose `Sweep`: `SweepInputFactory` + `SweepTransactionBuilder.BuildWithFee` at `SweepFeePolicy.Decide(IFeeService estimate)` + `Sign`), `WatchOutpointAction`, `StageWriteAction`, `RaiseChannelEventAction`, `AlertAction`. A row that names its resolving transaction is never built again (the monitor rebroadcasts, O6 bumps).
- Rows: one per output of ours on the commitment (`DelayedToLocal`, `LocalOfferedHtlc`, `LocalReceivedHtlc`), plus, when our HTLC-timeout/success confirms, one for its vout 0 (`DelayedToLocal` with the HTLC's direction/id, `OutputDescriptorData` with the `HtlcResolutionOutput` witness script, watched). The second-level sweep is decided by the parent HTLC row's plan. Anchor HTLC outputs are only logged (O7).
- Preimage rules (B5-LCL-RO-02): an incoming HTLC is claimed only with our own fulfill in its record (`Removal` Fulfill) or with the `KnownPreimage` of the outgoing HTLC that forwards it (`FindHtlcsByOriginAsync(Forwarded(channel, id))`, live or archived, checked against the hash) — the switch's upstream fulfill is refused once the channel is closed. A preimage seen on chain (the peer's direct claim of our offered HTLC, `HtlcWitnessParser`) is staged into the HTLC record's `KnownPreimage` (`IChannelStateDbRepository.ApplyAsync` with a one-record transition over `ChannelCommitments.Restore`, also put into the loaded model) in the same round as `OutgoingHtlcFulfilled` (I10), so the switch's own replays derive it too. When that stage is lost (no record, a failed save, a spend never reported) and a `LocalOfferedHtlc` output was spent by someone else, the per-block round fetches the spender through the optional `IBitcoinChainService` and reads its witness again (preimage → staged + fulfilled); only a witness that proves a path without the preimage (HTLC-timeout, revocation) may lead to the upstream fail. An unreadable spender never fails upstream: it is logged every round and alerted (`B5-LCL-LO-03`) once, at the reasonable depth. `StageKnownPreimageAsync` logs an error when the record is missing.
- Sweeps: an output is `Ignored` only when it cannot pay the floor fee plus a dust output (`ShutdownScriptValidator.GetDustThresholdSat` of the destination); when the capped fee (half the value) would leave less than dust, the fee is lowered to leave exactly dust. A builder refusal after that check is a bug: logged as an error, the row is left as it was and retried next block.
- Upstream events are raised again every round until the row is `Irrevocable` (the switch is idempotent; a refused upstream removal is retried each block). After that the switch covers it: the replayed upstream lock-in of a `Failed` circuit whose outgoing record derives no resolution (an `OnchainTimeout` is never persisted, or the outgoing channel is no longer loaded) fails the upstream HTLC with `permanent_channel_failure` (`HtlcSwitch.ResumeCircuitAsync`). Fail = `OutgoingHtlcFailed` with `HtlcRemoval.OnchainTimeout()` (`HtlcRemovalKind.OnchainTimeout = 4`, never persisted) once our HTLC-timeout is `ReasonableDepth` deep (`LocalCommitResolverOptions`, default 6), or, for an outgoing HTLC without an output in our commitment (trimmed, not yet in it, removed), once the commitment is that deep, at once when neither the peer's current nor next commitment has an output for it; a failure the peer had sent keeps its reason. `HtlcSwitch` turns `OnchainTimeout` into our own `permanent_channel_failure` for a forward, and `PaymentService.InterpretFailure` reports `PermanentChannelFailure` (no source index) for our own payment. Alerts come only from the spending witness (`OnOutputSpentAsync`).
- `Local/`: `LocalCommitResolverOptions`, `ISweepDestinationProvider` → `WalletSweepDestinationProvider` (an unused P2WPKH wallet address per sweep, handed to the monitor again), and `LocalCommitResolutionServiceCollectionExtensions.AddLocalCommitResolutionServices()` (TryAdd; the resolver as itself and as an `IOutputResolver` enumerable entry). The signer must keep the channel registered while it is `OnchainResolving`.

## Revoked commitment on chain: penalties (BOLT 5 plan O5-T2/O5-T3, ABCD W5-D; `Onchain/Resolvers/`)
- `RevokedCommitResolver : IOutputResolver` (Domain port of W5-A; `CanResolve` only `ChannelCloseKind.RevokedCommitment`), registered by `Onchain/Resolvers/Revoked/RevokedResolverServiceCollectionExtensions.AddRevokedCommitResolver()` (singleton, also as `IOutputResolver` through `TryAddEnumerable`, idempotent; needs `AddOnchainBitcoinServices`, the signer, key derivation, chain/wallet/fee services and `ISecretStorageServiceFactory`). It never locks, saves or publishes: the W5-A executor applies its actions in one save, then publishes and raises.
- Each round (`ResolveAsync`): `IRevokedCommitDataSource.LoadAsync` (`RevokedCommitDataSource`: channel from memory, else the database; the peer's secret n from the persisted shachain, point = secret·G; the revocation log entry and log start; the commitment tx from the block at `SpentAtHeight`, no txindex needed) → `ICommitmentOutputMapper.Map(..., CommitmentCase.Revoked, ...)` (from the log's spec; without an entry a no-HTLC spec with both balances at half the capacity, mapped by script; unmapped vouts → `AlertAction`, "pre-O1" when the number predates the log) → per output (`RevokedToLocal`, `RevokedHtlc`, `PaymentToRemote`) the row (created with `OutputDescriptorData` + a watch when missing), its recorded spend (the watched outpoint's spend first, `RevokedOutputSpend` with the spender's txid and the tx from its block or null when it cannot be fetched; the tx seen in `OnOutputSpentAsync` only while the row still says it was spent at that height, so a reorged-out spend is dropped; path and preimage from `HtlcWitnessParser`; ByUs = a stored broadcast of ours or the row's `ResolvingTransactionId`) and `OutputResolutionPlanner.Plan`. A spend that cannot be read is never guessed: ours is known from its txid, any other holds the output (a `B5-GEN-06` alert; its txid still invalidates a batch that spent it, and a second-level row already recorded for that txid is still penalized). Sweeps become `PenaltyNeed`s, `RaiseFulfilled`/`RaiseFailed` become `OutgoingHtlcFulfilled`/`OutgoingHtlcFailed` (removal `OnchainHtlcRemovals.OnchainTimeout()`, kind 4 = W5-B's `HtlcRemovalKind.OnchainTimeout`), raised **every round** the planner decides them (a failed executor save applies nothing; the switch is idempotent), one per HTLC per round, and never a fail after a fulfill was asked in this process; `AlertAction`s are returned once per process. The on-chain preimage is not persisted by the resolver (no repository API for it yet): after a restart the fulfill is rebuilt from the recorded spend. B5-REV-RES-03 applies only when every output of the revoked commitment is mapped: our offered HTLCs in the snapshot without an output then go through `PlanHtlcWithoutOutput` (fail at reasonable depth of the commitment, fulfill with a known preimage). While some output is unmapped (pre-log commitment, missing log entry, differing rebuild) any of them may be our offered HTLC, which the cheater can still claim with the preimage: the unmapped outputs are watched, a preimage in their spends fulfills at once, and the failure waits until every unmapped output is spent, reasonably deep, by a readable transaction without the preimage.
- The cheater's HTLC-timeout/success on a revoked HTLC output (`OnOutputSpentAsync`, or found again by `ResolveAsync`): a `RevokedSecondLevel` row for its output (vout = the spending input's index) with the `to_local`-form script (revocation key and the cheater's delayed key at the revoked point, `ChannelParams.Local.ToSelfDelay`; checked against the output's scriptPubKey, else an alert), a watch, deadline = its height + that delay, and at once an `OutgoingHtlcFulfilled` when its witness reveals our offered HTLC's preimage (B5-REV-07, B5-REV-RES-01).
- `Revoked/PenaltyTransactionComposer`: outputs without a live transaction share one `PenaltyTransactionBuilder.BuildBatched` penalty (with our `to_remote`), except those within `security_delay` (18) of their deadline (to_local and second level: confirmation + CSV; HTLC outputs: `cltv_expiry`), which get `BuildSingle`; a still-unconfirmed batch is split once any member comes within 18 blocks (each output alone, `ReplacesTransactionId` = the batch, the most urgent paying at least `SweepFeePolicy.GetReplacementFee` of the batch's fee); a batch that lost an input to the cheater (spent by another tx) is rebuilt for what it still has (B5-REV-09). The split is skipped (batch kept, `B5-REV-08` alert) when the batch's fee is unknown or the most urgent output cannot outbid it alone, since every single would then be refused as a replacement; the urgent single is emitted first, so the others no longer conflict once it replaced the batch. Fees: `IFeeService` estimate through `SweepFeePolicy.Decide` (floor 253, 50 % cap, 100 % for a penalty near its deadline; a single penalty whose fee would leave less than dust pays everything above dust instead); only an output that cannot pay its own fee at the floor becomes `Ignored` with an alert, any other builder refusal is alerted and retried next block. Signed with `ISweepTransactionBuilder.Sign` (revocation key: `SweepKeyKind.Revocation`), `BroadcastTransactionModel` purpose `Penalty` (`Sweep` for a lone `to_remote`), rows `Broadcast` with `ResolvingTransactionId`. RBF of a single penalty or batch, the per-target estimate (the earliest deadline among the needs sets the target, NL-296) and retiring a split batch's row are done by the O6 `SweepScheduler` (above).

## Adding an inbound channel message handler (the common task)
1. The message, payload and serializer must already exist. Check `src/NLightning.Domain/Protocol/Messages`, `src/NLightning.Infrastructure.Serialization/{Messages,Payloads,Tlv,Factories}` and `src/NLightning.Domain/Protocol/Constants/MessageTypes.cs`.
2. Create `Channels/Handlers/<Msg>MessageHandler.cs : IChannelMessageHandler<<Msg>Message>`. DI registers it automatically; don't add it by hand.
3. Add a `case MessageTypes.<X>:` to `ChannelManager.DispatchChannelMessageAsync`. It should call `ThrowIfUnknownChannelAsync` for a real channel id, then `GetChannelMessageHandler<T>(scope).HandleAsync(Cast<T>(message), ...)`. **If you skip this, the message falls into `default`, which throws a channel-scoped ChannelWarningException ("not supported yet"): the message is ignored, the peer gets a `warning` and stays connected.** That is the interim behavior for the dual-funding messages and closing_complete/closing_sig (N11); we never fail the channel for a missing handler, since LND would force-close it. A message for a channel we don't know at all (memory, temporary channels of the peer, DB) gets an `error` for that unknown channel_id (BOLT 1 SHOULD; `ThrowIfUnknownChannelAsync`).
4. At the top of the handler, check the state and throw ChannelErrorException or ChannelWarningException (`NLightning.Domain.Exceptions`) with `(message, channelId?, peerMessage?)`. The peer receives `PeerMessage` when it is non-empty; otherwise the internal `message` is sent (`src/NLightning.Infrastructure/Node/Services/PeerCommunicationService.cs`), so always pass a `peerMessage` if the internal text shouldn't leak.
5. Persist through a scoped `IUnitOfWork` (`ChannelDbRepository` then `SaveChangesAsync`) and update `IChannelMemoryRepository`.
6. Build outbound messages only through `IMessageFactory`. Return the replies to the requester as a list, in wire order. To send to a peer other than the requester (e.g. when forwarding), raise an event that ChannelManager converts into `OnResponseMessageReady` (see `FundingConfirmedMessageHandler`), synchronously while the channel's lock is held, so wire order equals persist order. `ChannelManager` already holds the lock of the message's channel id around your handler: don't acquire it again (it is not reentrant), and never take a second channel's lock from inside a handler.
7. Add tests in `test/NLightning.Application.Tests/Channels/Handlers/` (xunit.v3 + Moq). Existing handler tests are named `HandleAsync_Condition_Outcome`; PeerManager tests use `Given_X_When_Y_Then_Z`.

## Conventions
- Use a file-scoped namespace. Put System and Microsoft usings above it, and relative `using Domain.X;` usings after it.
- Store injected dependencies in `_camelCase` readonly fields. Guard hot-path logs with `if (_logger.IsEnabled(...))`.
- Lifetimes: managers are singletons. They create an `IServiceScope` per message or event. Handlers and `IUnitOfWork` are Scoped. Never inject IUnitOfWork into a singleton. `IChannelMemoryRepository` and `IUtxoMemoryRepository` are singletons.
- `dotnet format --verify-no-changes --exclude "**/BlazorTests/**"` is a CI gate. `.editorconfig` treats IDE0005/0051/0060/0044 and the naming rules as errors.

## Dependency rules
- The intended direction is Domain only. In practice the csproj also references `NLightning.Infrastructure` and `NLightning.Infrastructure.Bitcoin`, because of IBlockchainMonitor and IBitcoinWalletService (`Infrastructure.Bitcoin/Wallet/Interfaces`), the funding/commitment tx builders (`Infrastructure.Bitcoin/Builders/Interfaces`), and ITcpService, NewPeerConnectedEventArgs and PeerAddress (`Infrastructure/Transport`, `Infrastructure/Protocol/Models`). - New abstractions belong in Domain as interfaces (as `ISphinxService`/`IOnionReplayStore` already do). Their implementations go in Infrastructure(.Bitcoin).

## Gotchas
- Opening messages carry the **temporary** channel id. `currentState` only looks up real channels, so it is `None` for them, and the handlers must call `TryGetTemporaryChannelState(peer, id)` themselves. funding_created still carries the temp id. Only the initiator calls `UpgradeChannel`, which fires the `OnChannelUpgraded` event the Daemon's OpenChannelClientHandler waits on.
- `ForgetStaleChannels` only forgets fundee (non-initiator) channels still awaiting funding (`V1FundingSigned`/`ReadyForThem`) whose `FundingCreatedAtBlockHeight` is non-zero and at least 2016 blocks old. `FundingCreatedMessageHandler` sets that field to `IBlockchainMonitor.LastProcessedBlockHeight`; `HandleFundingConfirmationAsync` later overwrites it with the funding tx's first-seen height. Legacy rows with a zero height get it backfilled to the current block height on the next block, so their timeout starts then.
- `ConfirmUnconfirmedChannels` only re-drives `V1FundingSigned`/`ReadyForThem` channels whose watched funding tx is already `IsCompleted` (e.g. confirmed while offline). `HandleFundingConfirmationAsync` and `FundingConfirmedMessageHandler` ignore any other state, so channel_ready is sent once per confirmation. On reconnect, channel_ready is re-sent by the reestablish when both next_commitment_numbers are 1 (B2-RE-15).
- `AcceptChannel1MessageHandler` deliberately does not persist the initiator channel; `FundingSignedMessageHandler` saves it (V1FundingSigned, with its watches and the funding tx) before publishing the funding tx. On failure it removes the temporary channel by its temporary id (or the upgraded channel) and releases the locked UTXOs; failures in the validation checks before its `try` still rely on the Daemon's OpenChannelClientHandler to release them.
- Per-channel lock (NL-033): `ChannelManager` holds the lock for the message's channel id (the temporary id during the open flow, the real id after) around dispatch and the raising of the replies; block handlers (`ForgetStaleChannels`, the backfill, `HandleFundingConfirmationAsync` → `ConfirmFundingAsync`) take it per channel and re-check the channel's state under it. A channel is keyed by its temporary id until funding_created/funding_signed and by its real id afterwards; the per-peer inbound loop already serializes the open flow. The IPC open-channel operation takes it through `StartOpeningChannelAsync` (store the temporary channel + raise open_channel).
- scid aliases (NL-103): `FundingConfirmedMessageHandler` generates `LocalAliases` that avoid every real scid and alias in `IChannelMemoryRepository`, and reuses them if the channel already has some. `LocalAliases` is never persisted, so after a restart old aliases are forgotten: they are no longer recognized for incoming HTLCs and new ones are generated.
- Handler discovery uses reflection (`Assembly.GetTypes()`), which trimming or AOT may break.

With `acceptMultiPart`, any HTLC for a **Settled** invoice with the right secret and `total_msat <= AmountReceived` is accepted with `InvoiceAlreadySettled` (possibly a part of the set whose first fulfill settled the invoice, also one whose `amt_to_forward` alone equals `total_msat`), without the `cltv_expiry`-vs-height check, which depends on when the part is replayed (BOLT 4: MUST fulfill the entire set; a paid hash MAY be accepted, so a duplicate with the right secret is fulfilled too); without `acceptMultiPart` a settled invoice stays 0x400F. It never mutates the invoice. The check is a read, not a check-and-mark: the switch holds a per-payment-hash lock and settles the invoice in the first fulfill's unit of work, re-checking `Open` there.

- `Send/PaymentService : IPaymentService, IPaymentOutcomeHandler` (ABCD W2-C, N8-T3 + M4-T6; retries and MPP since ABCD W6-C, NL-270; singleton, registered by `Send/PaymentSendServiceCollectionExtensions.AddPaymentSendServices()`, which `AddApplicationServices` calls right after `AddHtlcSwitchServices()`; it also adds `IOptions<PaymentSendOptions>` (the daemon binds `Node:Payments`: fee limit max(0.5 %, 5000 msat) as CLN, `MaxParts` 16, `MaxAttempts` 32, `MinPartMsat` 10,000, `ExpiryTooSoonExtraBlocks` 6), `Routing/PaymentRoutePlanner` and `Send/PaymentOutcomeSwitchHandler`, the `ILocalPaymentHtlcHandler` through which the switch reaches `IPaymentOutcomeHandler`; it needs `ILightningSigner` to verify the `channel_update` of UPDATE failures). `PayInvoiceAsync(bolt11, amount, PayInvoiceOptions { Timeout, MaxFee, MaxParts }, ct)` → `PayInvoiceResult(Payment, Attempts, Parts)`; the `TimeSpan` overload is the same with the node's defaults. Validation as before (a decode failure, expiry, our own invoice, a missing/zero/different amount, a timeout <= 0 or a part limit outside 1..`PaymentSendOptions.MaxPartsLimit` (128) → `ArgumentException`; no processed block yet → `InvalidOperationException`; nothing persisted). Under the per-payment-hash lock (refcounted, one per hash): reconcile a stored `InFlight` payment without an HTLC id, refuse an `InFlight`/`Succeeded` hash **or one a call of this process is still paying** (its `PaymentSession`), then run rounds. The **session** (`Send/PaymentSession`, memory only, mutated under the hash lock) holds the parts (`PaymentPart`: our channel, the `PaymentRoute`, the persisted hops with their Sphinx shared secrets, the HTLC id), `Attempts` (offers, refused ones included), the deadline (= the timeout: no HTLC is offered after it), the terminal reason and the `Routing/RouteConstraints` learnt so far. A **round** (`RunRoundsAsync`): `PaymentRoutePlanner.TryPlan` for what the parts in flight do not carry, within what is left of the fee limit and the part/attempt budget, over every usable channel (`Open`, snapshot, link up; what one can send is the engine's own dry run `Send/LocalLiquidityEstimator` = binary search on `ChannelCommitments.SendAdd`, never persisted), onions (`PaymentOnionFactory`, `total_msat` = `PaymentRoute.TotalAmount`), persist, offer each part with `HtlcOrigin.Local(hash)` (a `CommitmentRefusedException` for a rule a smaller HTLC may pass (B2-ADD-S01..S04 balance/reserve/fees, S09 in-flight value, B2-DUST-03/04) bounds that channel below the refused amount; any other refusal (link down, HTLCs disabled, failed, shutting down, data loss, HTLC count) and a `KeyNotFoundException` avoid the channel for the rest of the payment; the round plans again; any other exception stops the payment unless channel memory shows the HTLC was added (`FindUnrecordedPartHtlc`)). **Persistence** (no schema change, one row per hash): the first round creates the row (`InFlight`, the amount, the round's fees, the first part's route and secrets; its HTLC id recorded in the next save); whenever every part failed, the row is saved `Failed` before a retry round replaces it (`AddAsync` over a failed row), so no crash leaves it `InFlight` without an HTLC; parts added while others are in flight live in memory only (their HTLCs carry the origin, so their outcomes still reach the payment after a restart, but their error onions can then not be decrypted). The row always records an offered part: when its part is refused by the engine or fails while others are in flight, `MovePrimaryPartAsync` rewrites it to a live part (route, secrets, HTLC id, the fees in flight); on success it holds the fulfilled part's route and HTLC and, as its fee, the fees of the parts in flight at the fulfill (the settled ones; failed parts cost nothing). The route and fee change only by replacing the row (`StageReplacementAsync`: the in-flight row failed, then `AddAsync`, one save). Outcomes while the session lives go to it: a fulfill with the right preimage succeeds the payment (the row gets the fulfilled HTLC's id if none is recorded); a part's failure is decrypted with that part's secrets (`DescribeFailure` + `FailureInterpreter`) and handed to `Send/PaymentRetryPolicy`, which learns (see its remarks: payee PERM or not understood → stop; `mpp_timeout` → retry; NODE failure of an intermediate hop → avoid the node; `fee_insufficient`/`incorrect_cltv_expiry`/`amount_below_minimum` → the hop's `channel_update` (our chain, the failed scid, the hop's direction, signed by the hop, not disabled, newer than one already used) becomes that channel's policy for this payment, else avoid the channel; `expiry_too_soon` → `ExpiryTooSoonExtraBlocks` more final CLTV; `temporary_channel_failure` → the channel may forward less than the failed HTLC; other channel failures → avoid the channel; unattributable, malformed from our peer or timed out on chain → avoid our channel); a retryable failure queues a round on the thread pool (`ScheduleRound`, even while other parts are in flight: partial-failure handling re-sends that part's amount), a permanent one stops new rounds. The payment is stored `Failed` (code, erring hop index and reason of the last failure, plus why it gave up: "Not retried: ..." or "Gave up after N HTLCs: ...") once no part is in flight and no round may run (stopped, no route left, attempts used, deadline, the caller's cancellation). The session ends (and the waiter returns) when the payment is `Succeeded` or finally `Failed`; a timeout returns the payment as stored while the session keeps handling its in-flight parts without new HTLCs. `WhenRoundsIdleAsync()` (internal) waits for queued rounds. **Without a session** (after a restart, or a hash this process never paid) the old rules apply (`MatchOutcomeAsync`): match by the recorded (channel, HTLC id), or, when none is recorded, the HTLC's stored origin must be `Local(hash)` or none and its record in channel memory must match the stored first hop; a failure is applied only when no other non-final outgoing HTLC carries the hash (`Pending`: another part of a split payment, or a retry, is live), and an HTLC whose stored origin is `Local(hash)` but that is not the recorded one (another part) fails the payment without a code once none is live. A fulfill with the right preimage that matches no in-flight attempt is logged at Error and still recorded as `Succeeded` (the preimage proves the payment; a stored origin other than `Local` makes it not ours). They save the outcome before returning (so call them before pruning on `OutgoingHtlcSettled`), and never take a channel lock. `IPaymentOutcomeHandler.ReconcileInFlightPaymentsAsync` (called at startup right after `PeerManager.StartAsync` registered the channels, by `NltgDaemonService` and the Docker `NLightningTestNode`): every `InFlight` payment without an HTLC id is attached to its HTLC when exactly one non-final outgoing HTLC in channel memory matches its first hop (or carries its `Local` origin), failed without a code ("never offered") when none does and no origin row sits on a channel that is not in memory, and otherwise left in flight; the same runs lazily when the hash is paid again.
- `Routing/PaymentRoutePlanner` (W6-C, pure; `TryPlan(PaymentPlanRequest, out parts, out reason)`): paths = every usable channel of ours to the payee, then to the first node of each route hint (`HintRouteBuilder.GetCandidates`, hint policies replaced by `RouteConstraints.PolicyOverrides`, avoided nodes/channels skipped, total CLTV within `MaxCltvExpiryDistance`); an amount fits a path when the fee is within the remaining limit, our HTLC is at most what the channel can send (given the parts already planned on it) and below its learnt local bound, and each hint channel forwards at least its `htlc_minimum_msat`, at most its `htlc_maximum_msat` and, with the other planned parts and the payment's parts still in flight over it (`PaymentPlanRequest.HintForwardsInFlightMsat`, from `PaymentRoutePlanner.SumHintForwards`), less than its learnt liquidity bound. ## HTLC switch (ABCD W2-B: ONION M4-T2 wiring, M4-T4, M4-T5, M4-T7; BOLT2 N8-T2)
`Payments/Switch/HtlcSwitch : IHtlcSwitch` (singleton), registered by `HtlcSwitchServiceCollectionExtensions.AddHtlcSwitchServices()` (called by `AddApplicationServices` right after `AddPaymentsServices()`, so production decorates the reestablish-gated probe), which `services.Replace`s the `LocalOnlyHtlcSwitch` and decorates the registered `IPeerLivenessProbe` with `LinkUpReplayingPeerLivenessProbe` (call it after `AddChannelOperationsServices()`/`AddPaymentsServices()` and any other probe registration; it throws when no probe is registered; idempotent). It works outside every channel lock and changes channels only through `IChannelOperations`; locks are taken in the order incoming HTLC (`KeyedAsyncLock<(ChannelId, ulong)>`) → payment hash (`KeyedAsyncLock<Hash>`) → channel (only inside `IChannelOperations` or the prune), never two channel locks. A `CommitmentRefusedException`/`KeyNotFoundException` (peer away, channel not loaded) is logged: nothing was persisted and the HTLC is still pending. The replays: `ChannelManager` at startup (every link is down then, so it resolves nothing that needs the peer) and `LinkUpEventReplayer`, which the decorated probe schedules on every `MarkLinkUp` (on the thread pool, without the caller's context: the snapshot and the unpruned settled rows are read under the channel lock, then `DerivePending` goes to the switch outside it; `WhenIdleAsync` for tests). A link is marked when a channel opens and after each channel_reestablish (N7, `ChannelManager.CompleteReestablishAsync`), which queues the channel's pending events itself; the decorated probe therefore skips the replay when the `ReestablishTracker` says the channel is `Reestablished`, so each link-up hands the events to the switch once (NL-264; without a registered tracker every `MarkLinkUp` replays).
- `IncomingHtlcLockedIn`: skipped unless the HTLC is still `RcvdAddAckRevocation` in memory. An HTLC with a `ForwardCircuitModel` is resumed, not peeled again: `Pending` → the channel HTLC that carries `HtlcOrigin.Forwarded(in)` (`FindHtlcsByOriginAsync`) is recorded (`Offered`), or, when there is none (the offer never persisted), the circuit is failed and the upstream HTLC gets `temporary_channel_failure`; `Failed` without an outgoing HTLC (refused offer) → fail upstream again; any circuit with an outgoing HTLC → the outgoing record (live, else its archived row from `LoadAsync`) resolves the upstream HTLC (preimage → fulfill, final failure → wrapped fail; then the archived row is pruned when the forward is done), otherwise it waits. Otherwise `IncomingOnionProcessor.ProcessAsync` runs with `replayOwner: null` (no replay check) when the HTLC's shared secret is already stored, else with `OnionReplayOwner(channelId, htlcId, cltvExpiry)` (processed before a restart or reestablish), the secret is stored (`RecordOnionSecretAsync`), then: malformed → `FailMalformedHtlcAsync`; failed → `FailHtlcAsync(CreateErrorPacket)`; final → receive; forward → forward.
- Receive (NL-253; `basic_mpp`, ABCD W6-B): under the payment-hash lock, an HTLC that was resolved while waiting for the lock (a set's fulfill or timeout acts on other parts under the hash lock, not their incoming locks) is skipped; then re-read the invoice and `FinalHopProcessor.Evaluate(..., acceptMultiPart)`; `InvoiceAlreadySettled` → fulfill at once (even at height 0); otherwise height 0 → `temporary_node_failure` (`acceptMultiPart` = `NodeOptions.Features.BasicMpp != No`, the default Optional). Every accepted HTLC joins the payment's in-memory `HtlcSet` (`Switch/HtlcSet.cs`, by payment hash; parts no longer `RcvdAddAckRevocation` are pruned first; a single-part payment is a set of one). A part whose `total_msat` differs from the set's fails the **whole** set with PERM|15 (BOLT 4 SHOULD). While the parts' `amt_to_forward` are below `total_msat` the set is held and a `TimeProvider` timer runs (`HtlcSwitchOptions.MppTimeout`, default 60 s from the first part, BOLT 4 SHOULD ≥ 60 s); when it fires (background round, `WhenIdleAsync` for tests) an incomplete set is failed with `mpp_timeout` (0x17); a part whose failure is refused (peer away) is remembered and failed with `mpp_timeout` on its replay. A complete set is fulfilled part by part (until the first fulfill goes through, a set that is no longer complete, a part resolved elsewhere, is held again): the first fulfill that goes through is `FulfillHtlcAsync(..., stageWithFulfill)` whose staging re-reads the invoice on the fulfill's unit of work, checks it is still `Open` (else `InvoiceNotOpenException`: nothing persisted, the whole set fails with PERM|15) and stages `Accept(sum of the held parts' amount_msat)` + `Settle` + `UpdateAsync` (one save: `Settled` means "the preimage is out"); the others are plain fulfills. A refused fulfill leaves its part locked in: its replay (link-up, startup) sees a `Settled` invoice and a multi-part HTLC (`InvoiceAlreadySettled`) and fulfills it, so the whole set is always fulfilled (BOLT 4 MUST). When every fulfill is refused nothing was revealed and the complete set stays held for the replays (the timeout never fails a complete set). No schema change: the set lives in memory and is rebuilt from the persisted incoming HTLCs by the startup and link-up replays after a restart (its timeout starts again). `AddHtlcSwitchServices` registers `HtlcSwitch` as its own singleton and `IHtlcSwitch` as a factory over it, so the container disposes it (timers stopped, timeout rounds canceled; `IDisposable` + `IAsyncDisposable`) also behind `DustExposureHtlcSwitch`. Without `basic_mpp` a second HTLC for a settled hash fails with PERM|15; the switch never writes `Accepted` on its own. Known limits: with `basic_mpp`, a late HTLC with the right secret for an already settled invoice is fulfilled (we cannot tell it from a held part of the set without persisting the set; the payer overpays itself, BOLT 4 MAY); a held part of a settled set whose channel is force-closed before its fulfill is not claimed on chain (the resolvers never use an invoice preimage alone; NL ledger follow-up).
- Forward: the onion's scid resolves to an `Open` channel by `LocalAliases`, `RemoteAlias`, or the real scid when `UseScidAlias` is `No`; `IForwardingPolicy` gets an `OutgoingChannelInfo` (usable = open, snapshot, no data loss, link up; `AvailableToSend` = settled local balance − our pending offered HTLCs − the reserve the peer requires). A policy failure is returned with our signed `channel_update` (`IChannelUpdateService.TryGetLocalChannelUpdate`, only when its scid is the onion's; else `len = 0`) for the UPDATE codes. Then the circuit is saved `Pending`, `OfferHtlcAsync(..., HtlcOrigin.Forwarded(in))` (origin persisted with the add), and the circuit marked `Offered`; when the offer throws (a refusal, or anything else except a cancellation) the channel state decides: an HTLC carrying the origin → the circuit is marked `Offered`; none → the circuit is `Failed` and the upstream HTLC gets `temporary_channel_failure` with the update (a cancellation leaves it `Pending` for the replay).
- `OutgoingHtlcFulfilled` (origin `Forwarded`): fulfill upstream at once, then mark the circuit `Fulfilled` (also when the upstream fulfill was refused: the next replay of the upstream lock-in fulfills from the kept outgoing record). `OutgoingHtlcFailed` (only raised when irrevocable): `WrapErrorPacket` with the incoming shared secret (circuit, stored secret, or a re-peel), or `CreateErrorPacketFromMalformed` for `update_fail_malformed_htlc` (a bad hash length falls back to `temporary_channel_failure`), then `FailHtlcAsync` upstream and the circuit `Failed`. Origin `Local`: every registered `ILocalPaymentHtlcHandler` (`HandleFulfilledAsync`/`HandleFailedAsync`; the W2-C `PaymentService` hook). No origin: logged.
- `OutgoingHtlcSettled` prunes the archived outgoing row (NL-243) only when nothing needs its replay: for a forward, the upstream HTLC is no longer `RcvdAddAckRevocation` in a **loaded** channel (an upstream channel not registered yet keeps the row) and the circuit is resolved; for a local payment, no handler failed on it in this process. `IncomingHtlcSettled` prunes the incoming archived row at once (nothing reads it after our removal is final; the startup replay also prunes rows left by older builds).

## Onion-routing (BOLT 4) hooks here
The onion library (M1+M2+M3) is used by `LocalOnlyHtlcSwitch` (peel + error onion, see above) and by `Payments/` (above: payload parsing, replay set, final hop, forwarding policy); wiring the payment core into the switch comes with ABCD W2-B. All of these are DI singletons:
- `ISphinxService` (`src/NLightning.Domain/Protocol/Onion/Interfaces/`, registered by `AddBitcoinInfrastructure`).
- `IHopPayloadSerializer` (`src/NLightning.Domain/Serialization/Interfaces/`, registered by `AddSerializationInfrastructureServices`).
- `IOnionReplayStore` (registered by `AddInfrastructureServices` as the persistent `PersistentOnionReplayStore`, NL-078; `IOnionReplayCache` was removed).
- `HopPayloadValidator` is a static Domain class.

`IHopPayloadSerializer` is declared in Domain (`src/NLightning.Domain/Serialization/Interfaces/`), so Application uses it without referencing Serialization.

The receive and send paths below are implemented by `IncomingOnionProcessor` and `PaymentOnionFactory`; they are kept as the reference call order.

Receive path (M4-T2, after the HTLC is irrevocably committed):
1. `var packet = new OnionPacket(add.Payload.OnionRoutingPacket.Span);`
2. `var peeled = sphinx.PeelAsLocalNode(packet, paymentHash, blindedPathTlv?.PathKey);`. Pass the update_add_htlc path_key only, never the payload's current_path_key. It throws `OnionException`:
   - A BADONION code (`ex.FailureCode.IsBadOnion()`) → `update_fail_malformed_htlc` with `ex.FailureData` (sha256_of_onion).
   - `InvalidOnionPayload` → `update_fail_htlc` encrypted with `ex.SharedSecret` (M3).
   - With a path_key, every failure comes back as `InvalidOnionBlinding`.
3. `if (!replayCache.TryAdd(packet.Hmac.Span))`: this is a replay, so fail it. Record the HMAC only after the peel succeeded.
4. `var payload = await hopPayloadSerializer.DeserializeAsync(peeled.Payload);`. Its offsets already count the stripped length prefix.
5. `HopPayloadValidator.Validate(payload, peeled.IsFinal, hasUpdateAddPathKey)`. When update_add_htlc carried a path_key, remap any `OnionException` from here on to `invalid_onion_blinding`.
6. If `peeled.IsFinal`, go to final-hop processing. Otherwise forward `peeled.NextPacket` (`.ToBytes()`) on the channel for `payload.ShortChannelId`.

Keep `peeled.SharedSecret` with the HTLC: failures need it later.

Send path (M4-T6):
- Serialize each `HopPayload` with `IHopPayloadSerializer.SerializeAsync`. That writes no length prefix, and `OnionHop` expects none.
- Call `sphinx.ConstructWithSharedSecrets(hops, sessionKey, paymentHash)` with a fresh CSPRNG session key.
- Keep the per-hop secrets for error decryption (M3).

Other hooks:
- The ChannelManager cases and handlers for UpdateAddHtlc, UpdateFulfillHtlc, UpdateFailHtlc, UpdateFailMalformedHtlc, CommitmentSigned, RevokeAndAck and UpdateFee exist (N6-T1); the onion is peeled only after `IncomingHtlcLockedIn` reaches the switch.
- Forwarding: resolve the next hop by ShortChannelId or its aliases through `IChannelMemoryRepository.FindChannels`. Then call `MessageFactory.CreateUpdateAddHtlcMessage`, which can't attach a BlindedPathTlv yet. Send the result through the `OnResponseMessageReady` event path.
- Failures: `CreateUpdateFailHtlcMessage` expects an already obfuscated `reason`, and no error-onion code exists yet (M3). `CreateUpdateFailMalformedHtlcMessage` takes sha256_of_onion plus a raw ushort failure code; cast from `FailureCode`.

## BOLT 5 remote-commitment resolution (plan O4, ABCD W5-C; `Onchain/Resolvers/`)
- `RemoteCommitResolver : IOutputResolver` (the shared port `Domain/Onchain/Interfaces/IOutputResolver.cs`; a stateless singleton registered by `Remote/RemoteCommitResolutionServiceCollectionExtensions.AddRemoteCommitResolutionServices()` together with `IRemoteSweepDestination` → `WalletRemoteSweepDestination` (an unused P2WPKH wallet address from a scope of its own, handed to the monitor), `IRemoteCommitmentSource` → `ChainRemoteCommitmentSource` (the commitment read from the block at `ChannelCloseModel.SpentAtHeight`, only for data loss or a rebuild whose txid differs) and `RemoteResolutionOptions` (`ReasonableDepth` 6, `IrrevocableDepth` 100); called from `AddApplicationServices` after `AddOnchainServices()`, options bound from `Node:Onchain` by the daemon) handles `ChannelCloseKind.RemoteCommitment`, `RemoteNextCommitment` (the snapshot's `RemoteNextCommit`, B5-RMT-01) and `FutureCommitment` (data loss, B5-RMT-03). It reads the channel, HTLC origins, circuits and payments through its own scope, never saves, never publishes and keeps no state: `ResolveAsync(close, outputs, height)` and `OnOutputSpentAsync(close, output, spender, height)` return `OutputResolverAction`s (row upserts, `ResolutionOutput` watches, `BroadcastAction`s, `StageWriteAction`s, `RaiseChannelEventAction`s, `AlertAction`s) that the executor applies in one save. Rows are created on the first round when the watcher did not write them (`OutputDescriptorData` with the peer's per-commitment point of that commitment; keys are re-derived by the signer). The resolver moves rows only between Pending, Waiting, Broadcast and Ignored; the executor marks a spent output Resolved and, 100 blocks on, Irrevocable. Alerts come from the spending witness (at spend time) and from the first data-loss round only.
- Rules: `to_remote` swept at once (D5; also after data loss through `ICommitmentOutputMapper.FindPaymentToRemote`); our offered HTLCs claimed with `<sig> <>` at `nLockTime = cltv_expiry` once the tip reaches it; the peer's offered HTLCs claimed with `<sig> <preimage>` before `cltv_expiry`, only when irrevocably committed (state >= 34) and only with an allowed preimage: our own fulfill of that HTLC (`HtlcRecord.Removal`), or the preimage its forward learnt downstream (the outgoing record, found through `FindHtlcsByOriginAsync(Forwarded(in))` or the circuit, live or archived: `KnownPreimage` or its fulfill). The switch cannot fulfill upstream once the channel is closed, so that downstream record is where the preimage is; never an invoice preimage alone (B5-LCL-RO-02; a final-hop HTLC whose fulfill was refused before the close is therefore not claimed). One transaction per output (fee from `IFeeService` through `SweepFeePolicy.Decide`; an output that does not pay its own fee is `Ignored`, but an ignored HTLC of ours still gets its upstream fulfill (a later preimage spend) or fail (as an HTLC without output)). The planner (`OutputResolutionPlanner`) decides every step.
- Upstream: a preimage of our offered HTLC seen on chain is staged into the HTLC's record (`HtlcRecord.KnownPreimage`, through `IChannelStateDbRepository.ApplyAsync`, and into the loaded channel's snapshot) in the round that raises `OutgoingHtlcFulfilled`, so the switch's own replays (`DerivePending` at link-up/startup) derive the fulfill too; our confirmed timeout claim (or an HTLC without output, B5-RMT-LO-03: at once when no other valid commitment has an output for it, else at `ReasonableDepth`) raises `OutgoingHtlcFailed` with `RemoteHtlcSwitchEvents.OnchainTimeoutKind` (= `HtlcRemovalKind.OnchainTimeout`), or with the peer's own failure when it had sent one. Both are raised again **every round** until the upstream actually has its removal: the incoming HTLC (origin `Forwarded`) has a removal, is final or gone, or its channel is not Open and the circuit is resolved; the payment (origin `Local`) is no longer in flight; an HTLC without a stored origin is raised until its row is irrevocable (the switch only logs a removal it cannot send).
- Data loss: every output of the commitment gets a row and a watch; `to_remote` is swept, the others are `OutputDescriptorKind.Unknown`, and any spend of them is searched (`HtlcWitnessParser.TryExtractPreimage`) for the payment hashes of our still-open offered HTLCs of the snapshot (fulfilled as above). Unknown rows become Ignored only once the commitment is irrevocable and every such HTLC is resolved upstream or expired `IrrevocableDepth` blocks ago.
